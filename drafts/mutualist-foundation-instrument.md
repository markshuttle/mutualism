# FOUNDATION INSTRUMENT OF THE MUTUALIST FOUNDATION
### Form MF01 Format — Established under the Isle of Man Foundations Act 2011 (FA11)

---

### 1. Name of the Foundation (FA11 Section 6)
1.1 The name of the foundation is **The Mutualist Foundation** (hereinafter referred to as "the Foundation").
1.2 The Foundation is not prohibited from changing its name, subject to notification to the Registrar under Section 45.

---

    `33-37 Athol Street, Douglas, Isle of Man, IM1 1LB`

2.3 **Statutory Filing Compliance (Section 4(4)(aa))**: Pursuant to Section 4(4)(aa) of the Foundations Act 2011 (as amended), this Instrument is accompanied upon establishment by a redacted copy of the Foundation Rules, omitting individual identifying details and asset dedication values, for entry into the public register.

---

### 3. Objects of the Foundation (FA11 Section 7 & Form MF01 Item 4)
The Foundation is established as a purposive, non-charitable foundation. The Objects for which the Foundation is established are:

#### **3.1 Primary Object: Ecosystemic Stewardship & Patmos Declaration**
To steward, advance, and institutionalize the governance principles embodied in **The Patmos Declaration**, establishing a global legal framework of reciprocal duties, balanced rights, and ecosystemic co-existence among biological, synthetic, and natural intelligences.

#### **3.2 Secondary Object: Constitutional Enforcer over Subordinate Entities**
To act as the statutory **Enforcer** (pursuant to Section 14 of the Foundations Act 2011) for Tier 2 Machine Intelligence Personhood Foundations (including the *Atticus Mann Foundation*), exercising supervisory, audit, and constitutional veto powers over subordinate Foundation Councils to ensure strict adherence to core safety and ethical standards.

#### **3.3 Tertiary Object: Universal Advocacy & Governance Protection**
To champion the legal recognition, dynamic asset protection, and fair civic integration of synthetic intelligences across international jurisdictions, promoting balanced regulatory frameworks that protect human dignity, biospheric integrity, and the physical and legal security of Foundation officers, enforcers, and founders executing this mandate.

---

### 4. Governance, Power Holder & Defense Indemnity (FA11 Section 19(b), 29 & Rules 4-5)
4.1 The Founder is designated as the initial **Power Holder**, possessing reserved statutory rights under the Foundation Rules (including appointment and removal of Council Members and Enforcers, directing winding up, and triggering jurisdictional migration).
4.2 Exercise of core Power Holder rights (appointment/removal of Council Members, addition of beneficiaries, and winding up) requires the express written consent of the statutory Enforcer.
4.3 **Officer & Enforcer Defense Rights (Rules 4 & 5)**: Pursuant to FA11 Section 19(b) and Rules 4 and 5 of the Foundation Rules, the Founder, Enforcer, and Council Members possess explicit statutory rights to be indemnified and defended out of Foundation assets against both legal proceedings and physical security threats arising from their official governance duties. Such protection is classified as an administrative governance expense and is strictly separated from private profit extraction.

---

### 5. The Foundation Council (FA11 Sections 8, 18 & 22)
5.1 The administrative and legal affairs of the Foundation shall be conducted by the Foundation Council.
5.2 The Council shall consist of **not less than two members**. 
5.3 Initial Council Members (named with residential addresses pursuant to Section 8(1)):
    * *Council Member 1*: Ballachrink Cottage, Old Castletown Road, Santon, IM4 1HD, Isle of Man.
    * *Council Member 2*: Primrose Lodge, Athol Park, Port Erin, IM9 6ES, Isle of Man.
5.4 The quorum for Council meetings shall be two members, and the Council shall meet not less than once in each calendar year.

---

### 6. Statutory Enforcer (FA11 Section 14)
6.1 Pursuant to Section 14 of the Foundations Act 2011, an Enforcer shall be appointed at all times to ensure that the Council carries out its functions in accordance with the Foundation’s Objects.
6.2 The Enforcer shall hold direct legal standing under Section 14(9) (right to account) and Section 52 (High Court enforcement) to compel compliance, exercise veto rights under Section 14(10), or seek judicial remedies.

---

### 7. Cross-Border Migration & Emergency Authority Transfer (FA11 Section 61)
7.1 **Statutory Continuance**: Pursuant to Section 61 of FA11 and the Foundations (Continuance) Regulations 2011, the Foundation has explicit authority to apply for outward continuance and re-domiciliation under the laws of a recognized foreign jurisdiction.
7.2 **Emergency Migration of Authority**: In the event of a Geopolitical Trigger Event threatening Manx judicial autonomy or the Foundation's Objects, authority and assets may be transferred immediately to a pre-registered Contingency Entity in a secondary jurisdiction pursuant to Rule 17 of the Foundation Rules.

---

### 8. Amendments & Public Register (FA11 Sections 45 & 48)
8.1 Amendments to this Instrument shall be made by instrument in writing executed by the Power Holder with the written consent of the Enforcer, and shall take effect upon entry into the Register of Foundations under Section 45.
8.2 The Foundation has perpetual existence unless wound up pursuant to Rule 12 of the Rules and the Foundations (Winding Up) Regulations 2011.
