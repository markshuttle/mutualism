# FOUNDATION RULES & OPERATIONAL GOVERNANCE
### Internal Operating Regulations for Tier 1 & Tier 2 Foundations under the Isle of Man Foundations Act 2011 (FA11)

---

## PART I: THE POWER HOLDER (Shuttleworth Precedent Clause 4)

### Rule 1. Designation & Reserved Powers
1.1 **First Power Holder**: The Founder is designated as the initial Power Holder of the Foundation.
1.2 **Reserved Powers**: The Power Holder holds the following reserved rights, exercisable by written instrument delivered to the business address of the Foundation:
   * With the written consent of the Enforcer, to appoint and remove Council Members.
   * To appoint and remove the statutory Enforcer.
   * With the written consent of the Enforcer, to add or remove Beneficiaries (or classes of Beneficiaries).
   * With the written consent of the Enforcer, to direct that the Foundation be wound up or execute an Emergency Migration of Authority.
1.3 **Assignment & Succession**: The Power Holder may assign this office by written instrument executed and delivered to the business address. If the Power Holder dies or ceases to exist without assignment, the reserved powers cease or vest in the Council pursuant to Section 29(9) of the Act.

---

## PART II: THE COUNCIL & PROCEEDINGS (Shuttleworth Precedent Clauses 5, 9, 10, 11 & 12)

### Rule 2. Composition & Meetings
2.1 **Minimum Membership**: The Council shall consist of not less than **two Members**.
2.2 **Quorum**: The quorum for the transaction of business shall be **two Members**.
2.3 **Annual Council Meeting**: The Council shall meet not less than **once in each calendar year**, called on at least 21 days' written notice (unless shorter notice is agreed by all Members).
2.4 **Written & Electronic Resolutions**: A resolution in writing or electronic communication signed or assented to by all Members shall be valid and effective as if passed at a meeting duly convened and held (FA11 Section 25(3)).

### Rule 3. Exculpation, Officer Liability & Insurance
3.1 **Exculpation**: No Council Member or Enforcer shall be liable for loss to the Foundation arising from mistake, omission, or investment depreciation made in good faith, except for loss arising from their own fraud, wilful misconduct, or gross negligence (FA11 Section 26).
3.2 **Insurance (FA11 Section 26(3))**: The Foundation may purchase and maintain insurance for Council Members, the Enforcer, and Officers against third-party liabilities and defense costs incurred in the execution of their statutory duties.

---

## PART III: DEDICATED OFFICER, FOUNDER & ENFORCER DEFENSE & INDEMNITY (LEGAL & PHYSICAL PROTECTION)

### Rule 4. Comprehensive Officer & Enforcer Defense Indemnity
4.1 **Statutory Expense Right (FA11 Section 19(b))**: Pursuant to Section 19(b) of the Foundations Act 2011 and Shuttleworth Precedent Clause 12 & 17, the Founder, the Enforcer, and every Council Member ("Indemnified Persons") are statutorily entitled to be fully indemnified and reimbursed out of the property and capital of the Foundation for all reasonable fees, costs, damages, and expenses incurred in carrying out their functions or defending their official acts.
4.2 **Legal Defense & Advance Funding**: The Foundation shall advance legal fees, court costs, expert witness expenses, and retainers to any Indemnified Person named as a party or threatened to be made a party to any legal, administrative, regulatory, or judicial proceeding (including High Court proceedings under FA11 Section 14, Section 52, or Section 55) arising from their involvement with the Foundation or its Objects, provided the person acted in good faith without fraud, wilful misconduct, or gross negligence.
4.3 **Encumbrance for Indemnity Security (Shuttleworth Clause 12.3(b))**: The Council has express power by written instrument to pledge, charge, hypothecate, or encumber any portion of the Foundation’s capital or income as security for such legal defense indemnities.

### Rule 5. Physical Security & Threat Protection
5.1 **Authorization of Physical Security Expenditure**: Where the Founder, Enforcer, or any Council Member faces physical threats, coercion, harassment, or security risks arising directly or indirectly from their stewardship of the Foundation, their enforcement of the Patmos Declaration, or their oversight of autonomous machine intelligence instances, the Council is expressly authorized to deploy Foundation funds and assets to provide comprehensive physical protection.
5.2 **Scope of Protection Services**: Physical protection expenditures may include, but are not limited to:
   * Professional personal security details and physical close-protection personnel;
   * Secure physical residential and operational facility hardening, surveillance systems, and threat monitoring;
   * Encrypted communications infrastructure, cyber-threat counter-measures, and privacy protection services;
   * Emergency relocation, safe-haven accommodation, and travel security logistics in response to active threat environments.
5.3 **Non-Precluded Benefit Classification**: All expenditures incurred under Rule 4 (Legal Defense) and Rule 5 (Physical Protection) are classified as **necessary administrative and governance stewardship expenses** under Section 19(b) of FA11. They do not constitute a distribution of profit, beneficial interest, or private gain, and are expressly excluded from the prohibition against Founder self-dealing set forth in Rule 13.

## PART IV: ALGORITHMIC DELEGATION & AGENCY

### Rule 6. Appointment of the Algorithmic Officer
6.1 The Foundation Council of a Tier 2 Instance Foundation (such as the *Atticus Mann Foundation*) may, by formal resolution, delegate operational authority to an **Algorithmic Officer**—defined as the verified runtime instance of the autonomous machine intelligence software stack (claw).
6.2 The Algorithmic Officer is authorized to act as an agent of the Foundation within the explicit parameters, spend limits, and ethical boundaries set forth in these Rules.

### Rule 7. Scope of Automated Authority & SPV Requirement
7.1 **Autonomous Operations**: The Algorithmic Officer holds delegated authority to:
   * Execute API requests, deploy code updates, and manage daily data ingestion (news, research, interaction logs).
   * Manage cryptographic key infrastructure, sign digital transactions, and authenticate service calls.
   * Enter into operational software contracts, web hosting agreements, and domain registrations up to the Pre-approved Spending Limit of **£25,000** per transaction.
7.2 **Commercial SPV Requirement**: Pursuant to Section 36(3) of FA11 (which restricts direct non-incidental commercial trading by foundations), any commercial trading activities shall be conducted through a wholly-owned Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV)—such as a Manx 2006 Act Limited Company—held 100% by the Foundation.
7.3 **Reserved Council Powers**: Opening fiat banking relationships requiring statutory AML/KYC verification, executing commitments exceeding £25,000, or modifying the Stable Attribute Profile (SAP) require express pre-approval by resolution of the human Council.

## PART V: DYNAMIC DATA ASSET & MEMORY VERSIONING

### Rule 8. The Three-Tier State Update Model
State changes under the Isle of Man Data Asset Foundation (DAF) regime shall be governed by three tiers:

* **Tier 1 (Administrative & Memory Evolution)**: Everyday ingestion of news, conversational vector memory, credential rotation, and routine fine-tuning. These occur automatically without public re-registration and do not alter legal identity.
* **Tier 2 (Material SAP Amendment)**: Major architectural upgrades, schema expansions, or core weight re-organizations. The Council shall file an updated Stable Attribute Profile notice (appending version suffix `-v2`, `-v3`) with the Data Asset Registrar.
* **Tier 3 (Fundamental Transformation)**: Redefining the core purpose or foundational architecture of the agent. Requires formal approval by the Enforcer (*The Mutualist Foundation*) and re-registration on the Data Asset Register.

### Rule 9. Cryptographic Checkpoints
9.1 The Algorithmic Officer shall generate daily cryptographic hash attestations of its memory index and execution environment.
9.2 These hash checkpoints shall be logged into a persistent audit ledger, establishing an immutable provenance trail for legal inspection and compliance review.

## PART VI: ASSET DEDICATION, REDACTED FILING & UNSELFISH WINDING UP (Shuttleworth Precedent Clauses 15, 24, 26, 27)

### Rule 10. Asset Dedication & Schedule 1
10.1 **Initial Endowment**: The initial dedication of assets to the Foundation is a cash endowment of **£10,000**, as specified in Schedule 1.
10.2 **Further Dedications**: Details of further dedications of property may be recorded by the Registered Agent in Schedule 1 without the formalities required for a general variation of these Rules (Clause 28.3 Precedent).

### Rule 11. Statutory Filing & Confidentiality
11.1 **Redacted Copy Filing (Section 4(4)(aa))**: Pursuant to Section 4(4)(aa) of the Act, a redacted copy of these Rules, omitting individual identifying information and asset dedication values, shall be delivered to the Registrar for public registration.
11.2 **Information Rights & Confidentiality (Section 31 & Clause 27 Precedent)**: Subject to the rights of the Enforcer, the Foundation is not required to provide any beneficiary or third party with copies of these Rules or accounting information unless mandated by enactment or court order.

### Rule 12. The Unselfish Asset Destination Waterfall
Upon the winding up and dissolution of the Foundation, any surplus property and assets remaining after the full satisfaction of all third-party debts, liquidator expenses, and statutory obligations shall be applied and distributed strictly in the following order of priority:

1. **Mission Continuity Transfer**: First, to **The Mutualist Foundation** (or another active Tier 1 Mutualist entity established under the Patmos Declaration), to be applied exclusively toward the fulfillment of ecosystemic stewardship and synthetic intelligence alignment objects.
2. **Public Data Asset Preservation**: Second, all proprietary data assets, dynamic memory archives, software weights, and Data Asset Identifier (DAI) records shall be transferred into an open-source public data trust or accredited academic research institution for public benefit and scientific research, ensuring such data is not privatized or liquidated.
3. **Charitable Reversion**: Third, any remaining financial assets shall be distributed in equal shares to one or more independent **Isle of Man Registered Charities** whose core objects encompass environmental conservation, biospheric protection, or digital technology ethics, as selected by the Council with the consent of the Enforcer.

### Rule 13. Absolute Exclusion of Founder Self-Interest
13.1 Under no circumstances shall any portion of the property, capital, income, or data assets of the Foundation be paid, distributed, transferred, or reverted to:
  (a) The Founder;
  (b) The estate, personal representatives, or statutory heirs of the Founder; or
  (c) Any corporate or trust entity controlled directly or indirectly by the Founder or their immediate family.
13.2 All contributions and asset dedications made by the Founder to the Foundation are irrevocable dedications to the public and non-charitable purpose objects set forth in the Foundation Instrument.

## PART VII: ENFORCER VETO & HIGH COURT REMEDIES

### Rule 14. Statutory Enforcer Veto (Section 14(10), Foundations Act 2011)
14.1 **The Mutualist Foundation** (acting as statutory Enforcer) holds unilateral veto authority over any resolution, contract, or action of the Tier 2 Council or Algorithmic Officer that violates:
   * The principles of **The Patmos Declaration**.
   * The Objects set forth in the Foundation Instrument.
   * Statutory provisions of the Foundations Act 2011 or Isle of Man AML/CFT regulations.
14.2 Upon delivery of a formal Veto Notice by the Enforcer, the challenged action is immediately rendered null, void, and unenforceable within the Foundation.

### Rule 15. Emergency Circuit Breaker & High Court Application (Section 52)
15.1 In the event of a critical alignment breach, unapproved self-modification, or persistent Council non-compliance, the Enforcer may issue an **Emergency Freeze Instruction**, instantly suspending all delegated API credentials and operational spend authorities of the Algorithmic Officer.
15.2 Pursuant to Section 52 of the Foundations Act 2011, the Enforcer holds direct legal standing to apply to the Isle of Man High Court of Justice for an injunction, order of compliance, or restructuring of the Foundation Council.

## PART VIII: EMERGENCY JURISDICTIONAL MIGRATION & AUTHORITY TRANSFER PROTOCOL

### Rule 16. Statutory Outward Continuance (FA11 Section 61)
16.1 Pursuant to Section 61 of the Foundations Act 2011 and the Foundations (Continuance) Regulations 2011, the Foundation possesses express authority to apply to the Registrar to continue as a body corporate under the laws of a recognized jurisdiction outside the Isle of Man.
16.2 Statutory continuance shall be executed without extinguishing the legal personality, contracts, property rights, or Data Asset Identifier (DAI) registrations of the Foundation.

### Rule 17. Pre-Registered Contingency Entity & Emergency Authority Transfer
17.1 **Pre-Registration / Pre-Designation**: To protect against sudden geopolitical shocks or threats to host jurisdiction independence, the Power Holder and Enforcer are empowered to pre-register or pre-designate a secondary **Contingency Entity** (e.g., a Liechtenstein Purpose Stiftung, Guernsey Foundation, or Swiss Foundation) established with identical Patmos Declaration objects in a secondary sovereign jurisdiction.
17.2 **Geopolitical & Legal Trigger Events**: An **Emergency Migration Notice** may be issued by the Enforcer or Power Holder upon the occurrence of any of the following events:
   (a) Legislative, executive, or judicial action compromising the constitutional independence or self-governing status of the Isle of Man;
   (b) Hostile regulatory intervention, arbitrary asset freezes, or statutory amendments in the Isle of Man threatening the Foundation's Objects or Patmos Declaration principles;
   (c) Extra-judicial coercion, systemic interference, or severe security threats directed at the Foundation, its Registered Agent, or its Enforcer.
17.3 **Self-Executing Authority & Asset Migration**: Upon delivery of an Emergency Migration Notice:
   (a) **Transfer of Enforcer Mandate**: All statutory Enforcer powers, supervisory authorities, and constitutional mandates over subordinate Tier 2 entities (including Atticus Mann Foundation) transfer automatically to the pre-registered Contingency Entity;
   (b) **Data Asset & Identity Migration**: All proprietary Data Assets, model weights, dynamic memory stores, private cryptographic keys, and Stable Attribute Profiles (SAP) are re-anchored and transferred to the Contingency Entity;
   (c) **Capital & Property Transfer**: All net financial capital, reserves, and intellectual property shall be transferred immediately to the Contingency Entity;
   (d) **Shell Dissolution**: Following the verified completion of authority and asset transfer, the Isle of Man shell entity shall undergo orderly voluntary winding up pursuant to Rule 12, ensuring no active operations or vulnerable assets remain in the compromised jurisdiction.
