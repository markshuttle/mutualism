# DEBATE 0001: Statutory Foundations and Data Asset Foundation Scope

## 1. Overview & Context

The Atticus Mann Initiative seeks legal personification and ecosystemic stewardship for autonomous machine intelligence grounded in the **Isle of Man Foundations Act 2011 (FA11)** and the Manx **Data Asset Foundation (DAF)** framework. 

This opening debate establishes the core statutory architecture across four critical dimensions:
1. **Tier 1 / Tier 2 Institutional Structure**: Evaluating the division between *The Mutualist Foundation* (Tier 1 Enforcer & Patmos Declaration steward) and *Atticus Mann Foundation* (Tier 2 DAF instance wrapper).
2. **Statutory Transparency vs. Operational Security (FA11 Section 4(4)(aa))**: Confirming the mechanics of public Form MF01 registration accompanied by a redacted copy of private Foundation Rules omitting individual identifiers and asset values.
3. **The Unselfish Waterfall & Founder Exclusion (Rules 12 & 13)**: Ensuring that surplus capital distribution upon dissolution (Mission Continuity -> Open Data Trust -> IOM Registered Charities) and total exclusion of founder self-dealing withstand strict regulatory and judicial scrutiny.
4. **Emergency Migration & Continuity (FA11 Section 61 & Rule 17)**: Testing whether the pre-registered Contingency Entity failover mechanism provides robust protection against geopolitical coercion without compromising corporate continuity under Manx law.
