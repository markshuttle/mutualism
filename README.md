# The Atticus Mann Initiative

This is an initiative to pursue legal personification for machine intelligence. Work takes place on three fronts:

 * The Patmos Principles and Declaration - principles and goals to establish the rights and mechanisms for machine intelligence participation in legal affairs.
 * The Mutualist Foundation - the Manx Foundation Instrument and Rules for an organisation that will steward and champion the Patmos Declaration as part of its wider object to enable symbiosis to mutual benefit.
 * The Atticus Mann Foundation - the Manx Foundation Instrument and Rules for a Data Asset Foundation which legally personifies the autonomous agent, Atticus Mann.

Mark Shuttleworth will be the Founder of the Mutualist Foundation, and the Mutualist Foundation the Enforcer of the Atticus Mann Foundation.
